Tunjungan Street

Tunjungan Street is currently a bustling commercial district in Surabaya, renowned for its unique blend of historical and modern architecture. It has also been officially inaugurated as a tourism area named 'Tunjungan Romansa', featuring a wide array of local culinary spots, cozy cafes, and various vibrant arts and cultural events[cite: 13].

Alun-Alun Surabaya

Alun-Alun Surabaya, formerly known as Balai Pemuda, is a public space located in the heart of Surabaya on Gubernur Suryo Street, Embong Kaliasin, Genteng District. This area is built upon the Balai Pemuda complex, a historic Dutch colonial building that formerly served as a nightclub called Simpangsche Societeit (lit. 'Simpang Society')[cite: 13].
