Welcome to my hometown

SURABAYA

Surabaya, the City of Heroes

that is how people call it. As the capital of East Java, it radiates a resilient spirit of struggle that has endured from the past to the present. It is a place where cultures do not merely coexist, but where a rich diversity blends and thrives amid the hustle and bustle of the city. From dawn until dusk, its charm captivates a million eyes with its vibrant contrasts and endless allure. It is easy to find smiles behind the crowd, easy to find a greeting, and easy to fall in love with Surabaya.

If any word belongs to Surabaya, it is perhaps 'sincerity'.
